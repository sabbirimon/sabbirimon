The silent valley

Rafi reaches the jungle after sundown. Pip, his pocket robot, points to a glow between the tents. Open Jungle Campfire and turn on Ambience to hear the wood crackle. Try the Sound mixer to bring the crickets in or out.

The rulebook

Practice points
Each distinct exercise earns one point. Each point in your personal-best quiz score also counts. Repeating an exercise gives you another chance to practise.
Badges and coins
Finish every exercise in a chapter to earn its badge. The mystery journal has 24 coins across six trails and saves finds in this browser.
Cloud and ranking
Sign in to sync supported learning progress. Joining the public leaderboard is optional. It lists up to 50 players by alias.
Make yourself comfortable
World Studio has motion, ambience, weather and scene controls. Open Privacy policy and cookie settings to review optional metrics.
Further adventures
Open the video player to watch a lesson from YouTube or Bilibili. Try the arcade, browser toolbox, AI Lab or Systems Lab. The site map lists the destinations.
