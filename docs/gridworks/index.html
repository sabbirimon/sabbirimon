YOUR FIRST TWO CONNECTIONS

Start small. Bring a city online.

Your first mission in Lantern Village needs just two buildings. The starter homes, data center and cables are already placed. Follow the yellow map markers, then watch the mission checklist.

01

POWER → Coal station → marker 1.

Open POWER in the building catalog and select Coal station. Click the yellow 1 at tile 14 / 12, just below the starter cables. The small “NOVA’S FIRST CONNECTION” button in MISSION also selects this tool for you.

02

DATA → 3G cell tower → marker 2.

Select 3G cell tower in DATA, then click yellow 2 at tile 15 / 12. The starter wiring supplies the network connection. Nearby homes should now receive power and wireless service.

03

Check MISSION. Hold it steady.

Watch the population, service and income goals turn green. Keep them satisfied for 12 simulation seconds to complete the first chapter. NEXT REGION opens the next city; WORLD shows your growing campaign and REGIONS lets you revisit unlocked cities.

04

Inspect, connect, then expand.

SELECT a building to inspect, repair or upgrade it. Use POWER, DATA and SIGNAL map overlays to diagnose missing connections. Add utility links and network cables before growing homes, businesses and compute services. SELL removes the selected asset for its displayed refund.

05

Set your prices. Save your city.

ISP / OPS controls service pricing and, in later chapters, your automated operators. Higher prices can reduce adoption, so watch operating income and service. Under MISSION, SAVE CITY saves manually; autosave also keeps progress in this browser. RESTART resets only the current city after confirmation.

BUTTONS AT A GLANCE

POWER / DATA / COMPUTE / CITY: choose what to build. SELECT: inspect assets. PAN: drag the map. + / − / FIT: zoom or frame the city.

Ⅱ / 1× / 2× / 4×: pause or change simulation speed. STORY: replay briefings. GUIDE: reopen this tutorial anytime. SOUND: optional game effects.

KEYBOARD & PHONE

Arrow keys Move map cursor Enter Build / inspect Esc Cancel tool

Space + drag Pan Scroll Zoom. On a phone, use BUILD to choose a tool, MAP to place it, and MISSION to see your goals.

Gridworks is a fictional infrastructure simulation. Cities, credits and operators exist only in this game; progress stays in this browser. The simulation pauses while this guide is open.
